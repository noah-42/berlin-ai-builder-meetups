# Berlin AI Builder Meetups - details and sources

Back to the [events table](README.md).

### What to know before registering

- **AI Builders November:** for developers, product people, ML practitioners, founders and active builders. The organizer recommends knowing concepts such as agents, RAG and MCP. The free-minimum Student / Bootstrapper option is not the general ticket; choose the appropriate type and verify its final terms. A sponsored option is also listed, subject to approval. Sessions may be filmed or streamed.
- **BLISS:** a research-focused evening, not a hands-on coding workshop. The talk covers chain-of-thought safety, security and monitoring, followed by questions and conversation.
- **Build x Agents:** SME-focused agent examples and discussion, not a coding hackathon. Hosted by Build X AI and Chris at Futura House. The organizer normally releases exact entry details after approval; confirm those before travel.
- **Daytona & Cognee:** particularly relevant to people building agents and deciding where their code should run. The listing runs until 21:00, while the written networking agenda ends at 20:30. Confirm the final schedule and approved venue with the host.

No application cutoff was published in the checked descriptions for these four events. Approval and capacity can change; an event page being live does not guarantee admission.

## Sources and scope

Organizer icons are the organizers' own brand images from the linked organizer sites and Luma organizer profiles; copies are stored in this repo and in FaviconHub.

Dates, ticket metadata, venues and entry notes were checked against the organizer's linked Luma/Eventbrite pages on 12 November 2026. AI Builders dates and event types were also checked against its [public Berlin event feed](https://aibuilders.club/api/public/events?city=BER). Prices shown are listed ticket amounts, not a checkout quote; extra charges, if any, must be checked before paying.

Discovery began with the [Orb catalog](https://orb8.net/catalog) and the Berlin events feed, then organizer pages. Blank-title records, duplicate listings, broad business networking and paid conference clusters were not treated as builder meetups. ai/nudge was excluded because its description explicitly says no tech or tools. This is a selected list, not a complete calendar of Berlin.
